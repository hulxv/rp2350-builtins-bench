- **legacy**: generic compiler-rt C (`<name>.c`).
- **libc**: LLVM-libc backed (`<name>.cpp`).

## Closed-world recursion probe

libc objects relinked so their internal soft-float calls (`__aeabi_fadd`, `__aeabi_dmul`, ...) bind to the libc builtins themselves, as on a soft-float target with no `arm/*.S` (e.g. riscv32 without F). `MSPLIM` turns unbounded recursion into a stack-overflow fault.

|---|---|
| `addsf3(1,1)` | returned `0x40000000` |
| `adddf3(1,1)` | returned `0x4000000000000000` |
| `adddf3(1,2^-60)` | **fault** (IPSR 3, CFSR `0x00100000`, STKOF: stack overflow) |
